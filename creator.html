What makes a good mystery?
It starts with a question and gives detectives clues to figure it out. The solution should make sense when the clues are put together.
Example
Where? School greenhouse
Problem? The plants wilt before a contest.
People? Student gardener, custodian.
Clues? Thermometer, watering schedule.
Twist? A broken sensor turned on the heater.
Not sure yet?
It's fine to leave some answers blank. Just describe the strange event as clearly as you can. Your teacher and the game builder can develop the characters, clues, and ending.
We will use original characters and settings. A favorite book can inspire the genre, but we won't copy its characters or story.
When you're done
Download the text proposal and submit it in Google Classroom. This site does not send it automatically to your teacher.
Your drafts save on this browser only. On shared Chromebooks, download your work and delete it afterward. Don't include classmates' real names or personal information.
